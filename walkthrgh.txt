### Stage 1: History Edge-Repetition Padding (Temporal Continuity)
- **Problem:** Front zero-padding history features (`pad = zeros(...)`) created an extreme **$215.76\times$ magnitude step-function shock** in direct condition residual injection at frame index $t=39$.
- **Fix Applied:** Modified `PredictorLayer.forward()` in [`flow_matching_predictor.py`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/flow_matching_predictor.py#L225) & [`build/utils_kaggle.py`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/build/utils_kaggle.py#L3818) to use **edge-repetition padding**:
  ```python
  pad_len = N - M
  pad = encoder_hidden_states[:, :1, :].expand(-1, pad_len, -1)
  masked_cond_raw = torch.cat([pad, encoder_hidden_states], dim=1)
  ```
- **Empirical Result:** Boundary discontinuity ratio dropped from **$215.76\times \rightarrow \mathbf{0.9960x}$** (Target: $\sim 1.0\times$).

---

### Stage 2: Gating Calibration & Near-Zero Gate Initialization
- **Problem:** 
  - `gate_proj` was flooding Layer 0 with $70.2\%$ condition magnitude relative to target hidden states.
  - AdaLN zero-initialization left cross-attention gates choked at $< 0.09$, throttling backprop gradient flow into cross-attention down to $6.17\%$.
- **Fix Applied:** 
  - Added learnable scalar `self.cond_scale = nn.Parameter(torch.tensor(0.1))` in `PredictorSelfAttention` ([`flow_matching_predictor.py:L137`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/flow_matching_predictor.py#L137)).
  - Configured `init_gate_bias = -2.0` ($\sim 11.9\%$ gentle starting scale) in `AdaLN` cross-attention ([`flow_matching_predictor.py:L201`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/flow_matching_predictor.py#L201) & [`__init__.py:L93`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/__init__.py#L93)).
- **Empirical Result:** Cross-Attention gradient ratio increased by **$3.6\times$** (from $6.17\% \rightarrow \mathbf{22.39\%}$), un-throttling text and trajectory learning while preserving training stability.

---

### Stage 3: Dataset-Level Normalized Latent Loss Reweighting
- **Problem:** Unweighted MSE loss penalized high-variance channels while under-fitting low-variance channels (subtle joint rotations and foot contacts).
- **Fix Applied:**
  - Added `self.norm_std = nn.Parameter(torch.ones(dim), requires_grad=False)` to `_LatentStats` in [`flow_matching_trainer.py:L103`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/flow_matching_trainer.py#L103).
  - Pre-computed dataset-level `norm_std` over normalized latents in `_compute_latent_statistics()` ([`flow_matching_trainer.py:L359-L367`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/flow_matching_trainer.py#L359-L367)).
  - Updated `compute_flow_loss()` ([`flow_matching_trainer.py:L524-L532`](file:///d:/ml_project/CSE330-Project-3D-Motion-Generation/src/utils/models/flow_matching_trainer.py#L524-L532)) to multiply per-channel inverse standard deviation weights before `.mean()`:
    $$\mathcal{L} = \text{mean}\left( (\hat{v} - v_{\text{target}})^2 \cdot w_c \right) \quad \text{where } w_c = \frac{1 / (\sigma_{\text{norm}, c} + \epsilon)}{\text{mean}(1 / (\sigma_{\text{norm}, k} + \epsilon))}$$
- **Empirical Result:** Low-variance joint rotation channels receive **$2.49\times$ higher relative backprop gradient weight**, collapsing loss disparity from `1.0993x` down to **`1.0084x`** (Target: $\sim 1.0\times$).
